1. MIDVALE CASTINGS LLC
Master Supply Agreement dated March 1, 2025, between Midvale Castings LLC, an Indiana limited liability company ("Buyer"), and Brickyard Industrial Supply, Inc., an Illinois corporation ("Supplier"). Term: thirty-six (36) months, renewing annually thereafter unless terminated on ninety (90) days' notice.

SECTION 6. STOCKED ITEMS.

6.1 Stocking Commitment. Supplier shall maintain at its Fort Wayne, Indiana branch, at all times during the Term, physical inventory of each item listed in Schedule 6-A (the "Stocked Items") in a quantity not less than the Minimum On-Hand Quantity set out for that item in Schedule 6-A. Inventory on order, in transit or held at any other Supplier location shall not count toward the Minimum On-Hand Quantity.

6.2 Delivery. Supplier shall deliver any Stocked Item ordered by Buyer before 2:00 p.m. local time on a business day to Buyer's Fort Wayne facility no later than the end of that business day, at no charge for freight.

6.3 Shortfall. If Supplier is unable to deliver a Stocked Item in accordance with Section 6.2, Buyer may procure a substitute item from any source and Supplier shall reimburse Buyer for the difference between the price paid and the Agreement price, plus fifteen percent (15%) of that difference as an administrative charge, together with any expedited freight incurred by Buyer. Two (2) shortfalls in any calendar quarter shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement.

6.4 Substitution. Supplier shall not substitute another item for a Stocked Item without Buyer's prior written approval, which Buyer may withhold in its sole discretion.

6.5 Schedule Changes. Buyer may add items to or remove items from Schedule 6-A on thirty (30) days' written notice. Supplier may request a change to a Minimum On-Hand Quantity, which Buyer will consider in good faith.

2. STARR RIDGE PACKAGING, INC.
Supply and Services Agreement dated August 18, 2025, between Starr Ridge Packaging, Inc., an Illinois corporation ("Customer"), and Brickyard Industrial Supply, Inc. ("Distributor"). Term: twenty-four (24) months.

ARTICLE 9. CONSIGNMENT-FREE STOCKING PROGRAM.

9.1 Distributor agrees to hold, at its Rockford, Illinois branch, a dedicated minimum quantity of each product identified in Exhibit C (each a "Program Item") for Customer's exclusive call-off. The minimum quantity for each Program Item (the "Minimum On-Hand Quantity") is stated in Exhibit C. Distributor's obligation under this Article 9 is continuous and applies on every day of the Term, including Distributor's annual inventory count.

9.2 Distributor shall make Program Items available for Customer pick-up at the Rockford branch within two (2) hours of Customer's request during Distributor's business hours, and shall deliver Program Items to Customer's Rockford plant within four (4) hours of a request made outside those hours, at Distributor's expense.

9.3 Where a Program Item is on hand in a quantity below its Minimum On-Hand Quantity for more than one (1) business day, Distributor shall notify Customer's Plant Manager in writing on the same day and shall, at Customer's election, either expedite replenishment at Distributor's cost or credit Customer an amount equal to five percent (5%) of the preceding month's purchases of that Program Item.

9.4 Distributor shall not discontinue, substitute or re-source a Program Item without sixty (60) days' prior written notice to Customer and Customer's written consent to the substitute.

9.5 Program Items are priced per Exhibit B and are not subject to Distributor's general price increases during the Term except as provided in Section 5.3.

3. LOCKPORT PRECISION COMPONENTS
Integrated Supply Agreement dated November 3, 2025, between Lockport Precision Components, LLC ("LPC") and Brickyard Industrial Supply, Inc. ("BIS"). Term: thirty-six (36) months.

SECTION 4. CRITICAL SPARES AND CONSUMABLES.

4.1 Designation. The items listed in Appendix 4.1 are designated Critical Items. BIS acknowledges that LPC's production depends on the uninterrupted availability of the Critical Items and that LPC has entered into this Agreement in reliance on the stocking commitment in this Section 4.

4.2 Stocking Level. BIS shall keep in stock at its Joliet, Illinois distribution center, at all times during the Term, not less than the quantity shown for each Critical Item in Appendix 4.1 (the "Minimum On-Hand Quantity"). BIS shall replenish each Critical Item upon any withdrawal so that the quantity on hand does not remain below the Minimum On-Hand Quantity for longer than two (2) business days.

4.3 Same-Day Service. BIS shall deliver Critical Items to LPC's Lockport facility within four (4) hours of LPC's request, twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week, at no additional charge.

4.4 Remedies. In the event of a failure under Section 4.2 or 4.3, LPC may, in addition to any other remedy, (a) charge BIS for documented production downtime at LPC's standard rate of $2,400 per hour, up to a maximum of $24,000 per occurrence, and (b) after the second such failure in any twelve (12) month period, terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days' notice without liability.

4.5 Reporting. BIS shall provide LPC, on the first business day of each month, a report of on-hand quantities of each Critical Item as of the last day of the preceding month, certified by BIS's Purchasing Manager.
