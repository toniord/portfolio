## Firm Overview

(confidence: high | source: public document — firm press release, revelarcapital.com, October 15, 2025, corroborated by ADV registered entity name change, 9at.com aggregator of SEC IAPD data, accessed June 12, 2026) The firm states that the rebrand reflects its "longstanding focus on uncovering potential and driving long term value creation" and a "new generation of leadership"; this characterization is the firm's own account and is not independently corroborated. (confidence: medium | source: public document — firm press release, October 15, 2025, self-reported)

## Investment Strategy

Revelar states it invests exclusively in Healthcare, Essential Services, and Specialty Manufacturing sectors, targeting North America-based middle-market businesses. (confidence: medium | source: public document — firm approach page, revelarcapital.com/approach/, accessed June 12, 2026, self-reported) An earlier formulation in the rebrand press release described the sectors as "healthcare services, business services, and specialty manufacturing," substituting "business services" for "Essential Services." (confidence: medium | source: public document — firm press release, October 15, 2025, self-reported) The current website and all post-rebrand press releases use "Essential Services." The divergence in terminology across firm documents is unresolved; the current approach page language is treated as the operative description.

The firm does not publish a stated deal size range or explicit revenue or EBITDA targets on its public website or in any press release reviewed. Based on the six disclosed platform investments made between approximately April 2024 and April 2026 (Batteries Plus, Pinnacle Service Brands, Summit Spine, CNS Cares, Lettermen's Energy, GSMS), deal sizes appear to span a wide range. The GSMS press release references approximately $1 billion of invested capital across six platforms, implying an average of roughly $167 million per deal, but individual deal sizes are not disclosed. (confidence: low | source: public inference — based on GSMS press release self-reported aggregate figure and six disclosed platform investments, April 2026; individual deal sizes unavailable)

The firm states a preference for control buyouts and characterizes its capital structures as "conservative." (confidence: medium | source: public document — firm approach page, revelarcapital.com/approach/, accessed June 12, 2026, self-reported) The Batteries Plus investment was structured as junior capital in an equity recapitalization with Freeman Spogli retaining a position, not a control buyout. The margin premium over general home health comparables reflects the federal reimbursement characteristics of the EEOICPA and Black Lung programs, which carry published rates, low price negotiation risk, and a captive beneficiary population. These are not financial data. (confidence: low | source: private inference — estimate from observable public signals; no financial data publicly available)
