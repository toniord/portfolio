# QofAI internship

Three agents I built as an intern at QofAI in 2026. I still work with QofAI today. Each folder
describes what the agent does and how it is built, with screenshots of real output.

- [`deck-generator/`](deck-generator/) generates proposal and status decks and gives a reviewer
  a studio to finish them.
- [`content-calendar/`](content-calendar/) plans a month of social posts per author and runs
  every post through a human approval queue.
- [`pe-research-agent/`](pe-research-agent/) writes a sourced research dossier on a private
  equity firm, with a confidence band and a source type on every claim.

## Why the code is not here

The code belongs to QofAI. I have prepared a sanitized copy of all three agents, with
everything specific to QofAI's business, clients, strategy and internal systems removed or
replaced with invented examples. That copy is currently under review by QofAI's engineering
lead, and it stays private until QofAI approves its release. Until then I am happy to walk
through the architecture and implementation live.

Every screenshot comes from an invented company or blog. The decks and calendar are real
pipeline output; the data behind them is made up.
